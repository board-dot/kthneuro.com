About Us

Our vision

KTH Neuro aims to create a community where students and professionals passionate about anything neuro, be it neuroscience or neurotechnology, can meet, connect, learn, and build together.

The crossroads

Neurotechnology sits at the crossroads of many disciplines: electrical engineering, medical engineering, biotechnology, computer science, neuroscience, and more. We believe that a dedicated space where these worlds intersect can spark transformative ideas, empower collaboration, and allow members to apply their unique skill sets in new and exciting ways.

Connection

At the heart of our vision is the belief that genuine human connection fuels innovation.

By fostering an inclusive community built on curiosity and empathy, we hope to create an environment where members learn to see through each other's perspectives, engineers understanding the needs of neuroscientists, designers learning from biotechnologists, and vice versa. This mutual understanding is what allows truly interdisciplinary ideas to flourish.

Bridges

Our goal is not only to build knowledge but to build bridges, between disciplines, between people, and between academia and industry. We are here to turn curiosity into collaboration, and collaboration into impact.
