What We Do

Three ways we grow

We bring our vision to life through a mix of hands-on learning, collaboration, and industry engagement.

01

Workshops, lectures, and projects

Learn by Doing

Through workshops, guest lectures, and projects, members gain exposure to the foundations and frontiers of neurotechnology, from brain–computer interfaces and neural signal processing to neuroprosthetics and computational neuroscience.

  • Curiosity-driven, accessible learning
  • Foundations to frontiers of neurotech
  • Hands-on workshops and guided projects

Foundations → Frontiers

02

Interdisciplinary projects and collaboration

Build Together

Innovation doesn't happen in isolation. By creating interdisciplinary teams, we encourage members to collaborate on projects, share expertise, and prototype creative solutions to real-world challenges in neurotechnology.

  • Form interdisciplinary teams
  • Share expertise across fields
  • A place to test, fail, learn, and grow

Prototype → Iterate

03

Companies, research labs, and startups

Connect with Industry

We collaborate with innovative companies, research labs, and start-ups to expose our members to the vast industrial and research applications of neurotechnology. These partnerships provide invaluable insights into the needs, challenges, and opportunities that exist beyond academia.

  • Company visits and guest speakers
  • Research lab collaborations
  • Real-world challenges and opportunities

Academia → Industry
