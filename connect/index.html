KTH Neuro is where students and professionals passionate about anything neuro meet, connect, learn, and build together. Here's what we do — and how you can be part of it.

What we do

01

Learn by Doing

Workshops, guest lectures, and hands-on projects covering brain–computer interfaces, neural signal processing, neuroprosthetics, and computational neuroscience. Everything is accessible and curiosity-driven — no prior experience needed.

02

Build Together

Interdisciplinary teams collaborate on real neurotechnology challenges, prototyping creative solutions together. A place to test, fail, learn, and grow with people from every field.

03

Connect with Industry

We partner with innovative companies, research labs, and start-ups to expose members to industrial and research applications of neurotechnology beyond academia.
